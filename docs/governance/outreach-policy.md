# Outreach policy

## The rule

**The discovery service never sends outreach.** It drafts a one-to-one
briefing; a person verifies the evidence, the recipient, the proposition and
the applicable rules, then sends from a normal business channel.

`HUMAN_APPROVAL_BEFORE_OUTREACH` is `true` and is not a performance knob.

## Preconditions

Before any contact:

1. The candidate passed the hard gate (official attribution + technical
   verification or two independent source families).
2. Every open `identity_mismatch` and `chain_id_collision` review task is
   resolved.
3. Any A1/A2 Africa claim is backed by quoted official evidence that the
   analyst has actually read.
4. The contact is not suppressed.
5. The channel is a public business route, preferred in this order: partnership
   or contact form → role mailbox → programme application → named individual
   with publicly disclosed business contact details.

## Content standards

- Truthful headers and subject lines; clear identification of who is writing
  and why.
- A postal address where required.
- A working opt-out, honoured promptly and recorded as suppression.
- Specific to the detected need. If the briefing could be sent to any chain,
  it should not be sent to this one.

## Frequency

- One approach per organization per opportunity.
- No follow-up without a new, material trigger.
- No parallel approaches to several people at the same organization.
- Volume is never a target. Relevance and trust are.

## Prohibited

- Automated mass sending from the discovery service
- Any contact with a suppressed channel
- Contact based on an A3 label presented as though it were project intent
- Inferring sale or acquisition interest — `investment_ma` requires an explicit
  public statement
- Purchased lists

## Complaints

Route to the outreach owner, suppress immediately on request, and record the
outcome in the workflow state. A complaint is a signal about targeting quality,
not just a compliance event: review what promoted that candidate.
